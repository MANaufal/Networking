>Signal regenerator

- **L1 (Physical) device** — works with raw bits/signals only
- Signals **weaken over distance** (attenuation) — a repeater receives the weak/degraded signal and re-transmits it at full strength
- Used to **extend cable distance limits** (e.g. past the 100 m Ethernet limit)
- Only **2 ports**: signal in, signal out
- No intelligence — doesn't read addresses or filter anything, just boosts bits
- A repeater with more ports = [[Hubs|Hub]]
