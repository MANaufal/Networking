>Faciliates communication **between** a network

> Routers are connected to a network
- Routers have an IP address and a MAC address just **like hosts**

>Routers forward packets not destined to themselves **unlike hosts**
>
>Routers are often arranged in a [[Hierarchy]] to improve scalability and simplify routing.

>Routers maintain a map of all the Networks they know about which is **[[Routing Table]]**
